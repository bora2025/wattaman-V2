# Publisher Marketplace Support and Escalation

| Issue | Primary owner | Escalation | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core/API defect | Wattanam Platform | Engineering lead | Critical 1 hour; high 1 business day |
| Marketplace account, review, download, or statement | Wattanam Marketplace Operations | Platform/Security/Finance by type | High 1 business day; normal 3 business days |
| Plugin behavior, documentation, or compatibility | Publisher Support | Wattanam Marketplace Operations | High 1 business day; normal 3 business days |
| Payment/refund status | Wattanam Customer Support | Finance Operations | 2 business days |
| Publisher revenue, tax, payout | Publisher Finance contact | Wattanam Finance Operations | 3 business days |
| Vulnerability or active exploitation | Wattanam Security | Incident commander, publisher security contact | Critical 1 hour; high 4 hours |

Support records identify customer impact, school-safe workaround, owner, severity, timestamps, plugin/release, related order only when needed, and resolution. Never request a school database or private signing key. Logs and support bundles must be redacted and owner-approved.

Refund responsibility follows cause: platform/marketplace failure is owned by Wattanam; plugin misrepresentation or defect is allocated to the publisher ledger; payment-provider reversal follows the verified dispute record. Customer resolution is not delayed while internal responsibility is decided.
