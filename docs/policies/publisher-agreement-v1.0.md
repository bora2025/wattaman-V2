# Wattanam Publisher Agreement — Version 1.0

Status: engineering publication candidate. Effective only after Product Owner and legal approval.

Publishers warrant that they own or are authorized to distribute every submitted component, name, trademark, translation, and data set. A publisher must maintain accurate organization, security, tax, payment, and support contacts; protect signing keys with MFA and dual control; submit immutable signed packages; disclose dependencies, permissions, network behavior, data use, migrations, licensing, pricing, and material limitations; and promptly report vulnerabilities or loss of control.

Wattanam may analyze, test, reject, suspend, withdraw, or emergency-disable a release to protect schools. Review does not transfer responsibility for the plugin to Wattanam. Publishers must support every published release for its stated window, cooperate in incidents and refunds, preserve required records, and provide a safe migration or data-export path when abandoning a plugin.

Acceptance is version-specific and retained with publisher, accepting account, timestamp, IP, user agent, and document SHA-256. A new effective version requires new acceptance before submission.
