# Publisher Acceptable Use — Version 1.0

Status: engineering publication candidate. Effective only after Product Owner and legal approval.

Plugins must not contain malware, hidden command execution, credential harvesting, surveillance, deceptive interfaces, unauthorized advertising, undisclosed telemetry, discriminatory automation, copyright infringement, or mechanisms that bypass Wattanam permissions, licensing, review, updates, or audit controls. Plugins may not export student or school data without a documented school-authorized purpose and explicit configuration.

Every capability must be necessary and declared. Network destinations, subprocess use, native code, destructive migrations, secrets, and high-risk data access require explicit review. Obfuscated behavior or a materially incomplete submission is grounds for rejection. Critical exploitation, active abuse, or publisher-key compromise permits immediate withdrawal and emergency disable while preserving school data.
