# Customer Terms of Service — Version 1.0

Status: engineering publication candidate. Effective only after Legal/Finance/Product Owner approval; tax content below is a technical requirement, not tax advice, and requires jurisdiction-specific legal review once DEC-005 fixes the launch region.

Wattanam licenses the core school platform under a proprietary commercial distribution ([ADR-0006](../architecture/adr/0006-licensing.md), DEC-001); the school does not receive source-code rights but retains an unconditional contractual right to export its own data at any time, regardless of subscription status, matching [privacy-policy-v1.0.md](./privacy-policy-v1.0.md)'s export rule. A subscription (DEC-002: annual, for updates and support) governs eligibility for new versions and official paid plugins; an installed core system remains usable after subscription expiry — expiry ends updates and support, it never erases data or disables read/export access, per [ADR-0006](../architecture/adr/0006-licensing.md)'s entitlement contract.

**Service description:** the school receives an independently deployable installation ([ADR-0001](../architecture/adr/0001-deployment-hosting.md)) it or Wattanam operates; Wattanam's marketplace identity, billing, and plugin-distribution systems are separate from the school's own data and never import it ([ADR-0002](../architecture/adr/0002-tenancy.md)).

**Invoicing and tax (technical requirement, not a rate schedule):** every order/invoice must record amounts in integer minor units plus an explicit ISO currency code (already an enforced commerce invariant, §11.3 of the master plan), must show whether the stated amount is tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive, and must retain an immutable invoice record for as long as the applicable jurisdiction's record-keeping law requires. The actual tax rate, withholding treatment, and invoice-numbering scheme are jurisdiction-specific and are explicitly **not** decided by this document — they require Finance/Legal sign-off once DEC-005 fixes the launch region, and engineering work to represent them in the commerce ledger once decided.

**Availability:** the core school system is designed to keep operating without continuous internet connectivity (DEC-006); Wattanam does not warrant marketplace/update/entitlement-refresh availability during an outage, but warrants that such an outage does not disable functionality the school already has ([ADR-0006](../architecture/adr/0006-licensing.md)).

**Termination:** either party may terminate a subscription per the commercial agreement; termination does not retroactively revoke data the school already holds locally, and export rights survive termination.

Acceptance is version-specific: a customer's acceptance of this document is recorded with account, timestamp, and document version, matching the acceptance-record pattern already built for publisher agreements ([publisher-agreement-v1.0.md](./publisher-agreement-v1.0.md)) — no equivalent customer-facing acceptance-capture mechanism exists in code yet; building it is engineering work this document identifies as required.
