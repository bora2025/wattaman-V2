# School and Customer Privacy Policy — Version 1.0

Status: engineering publication candidate. Effective only after Legal/Finance/Product Owner approval, and only once DEC-005 (launch region) fixes the applicable jurisdiction.

Wattanam processes school data (student, staff, family, academic, attendance, financial, and communication records) solely to operate the school's own installation; it is never used for advertising, unrelated model training, or shared across schools, matching the standard already published for third-party plugins. [data-classification.md](../architecture/data-classification.md) is this policy's technical substrate: its five-tier scheme (Public, Internal, Personal, Sensitive Personal, Secret) determines the handling rule below, and any new sensitive field is classified there before this policy needs amending. Most data subjects in this system are minors; every rule below defaults to the stricter interpretation on that basis, not the lenient one.

**Retention (proposed default, pending regulatory confirmation under DEC-005):** Tier 2/3 records are retained for the duration of active enrollment or employment plus 7 years afterward, matching common academic-record retention norms, unless local law requires a different period once the launch jurisdiction is fixed. Tier 4 credentials follow [secrets-inventory.md](../operations/secrets-inventory.md)'s expiry/rotation rules, not this schedule. A school closing its installation entirely may request full deletion, subject to the backup-retention window in [support-matrix.md](../architecture/support-matrix.md).

**Export:** A school owner may request an export of the school's own data at any time; a parent/guardian may request an export of their own child's record. Today this is fulfilled by an administrator manually producing the export — no self-service export tool exists yet for individual student/family records (the existing backup/restore capability, `OPS-003`, exports the entire database, not a scoped individual record, and is an administrator function, not a customer-facing one). Building scoped self-service export is engineering work this policy identifies as required, not work this policy performs.

**Deletion:** A departed student/staff record may be anonymized or deleted after the retention window above, except where a Tier 3 audit-trail obligation requires the underlying event to persist (in which case the record is anonymized — actor/subject identifiers removed — rather than the audit entry itself being deleted, per [data-classification.md](../architecture/data-classification.md)'s note that `AuditLog` inherits the sensitivity of whatever it snapshots).

**Bulk/administrative export restriction:** Per data-classification.md's Tier 3 rule, no bulk export of sensitive-personal fields (attendance, grades, fees, disciplinary notes) is permitted without a specific, logged justification — a parent exporting their own child's record is always permitted; an administrator exporting "all students" for a non-audit purpose is not, regardless of role.

**Known gaps this policy does not resolve:** `RefreshToken.token`, `ClassRegistration.generatedPassword`, and `MarketplaceAccount.mfaSecret` are stored in plaintext today (see [secrets-inventory.md](../operations/secrets-inventory.md)) — this is an engineering remediation item, not something a privacy policy can retroactively fix by declaration.
