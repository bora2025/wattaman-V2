# Organizing Management

Workforce and attendance management for companies, shops, stores, and other organizations.

## Implemented workflow

1. Configure the organization name, business type, and timezone.
2. Define job positions and responsibilities.
3. Add employers, employees, contractors, and interns, each with a stable QR attendance identity.
4. Plan holidays, company events, paydays, meetings, and reminders in the HR calendar.
5. Submit day-off requests and record a final approved, rejected, or cancelled decision.
6. Monitor workforce and daily attendance from the main dashboard.
7. Configure monthly, daily, or hourly base pay for each workforce member.
8. Record rewards, seniority bonuses, year-end bonuses, salary deductions, and wage deductions with reasons.
9. Generate draft payroll from the attendance period and applicable compensation adjustments.
10. Submit and review permissions for late arrival, early departure, short leave, or remote work.
11. Record disciplinary actions with incident evidence and effective dates.
12. Promote employees or employers into an active position while retaining their previous position and salary history.

Payroll stores present, absent, approved-permission and leave counts, worked hours, earning lines, and deduction lines used by each calculation. Approved permission dates are excluded from monthly absence deductions. Employee ID-card design, QR scanning, attendance reports, and corrections are the next modules to build on this workflow.
