🛰️ Attendance Stations

Approve the tablets and PCs that take attendance, and choose what each one accepts.

To add a station, open Attendance Station on the device itself and ask for approval; it then appears here.

Add a terminal

A face or card terminal (or a small program next to it) sends its events with a device key. Faces recognised by the terminal are sent as the student ID; cards as the card number.
